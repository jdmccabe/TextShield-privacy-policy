Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 9, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how PhoneHacks ("we," "us," or "our") handles user data for the TextShield mobile application (the "App"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring that your personal information is handled securely and transparently.

2. Data Access & Permissions

To provide messaging services, the App requires access to specific types of data on your device. We do not collect or transmit any of this data off your device.

  • SMS & MMS: The App reads and sends messages so you can use it as your messaging client. Your message history remains on your device.
  • Contacts: The App accesses your contact list to display names and photos for your conversations. We do not upload your contacts to any servers.
  • Photos & Media: The App accesses your storage only when you choose to attach a file or photo to a message. These files are processed locally for the purpose of sending via MMS.

3. No Data Collection or Sharing

We do not have servers to store your data. We do not collect, share, or sell any personally identifiable information, message content, or contact details to third parties. All data processing happens entirely on your physical device.

4. Data Retention

Because no data is transmitted to us, we do not retain any of your information. All data is managed by the Android operating system and is removed if the App is uninstalled or if you clear the App's data in your device settings.

5. Children’s Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any personal information from children. Since no data is transmitted off-device, the App is safe for all users in accordance with local privacy laws.

The App is designed as a local SMS utility. To provide its core functionality, the App requires access to your device’s SMS permissions.

3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not share, sell, or trade any user data with third parties. Because no data is collected or transmitted off the device, there is no data available to be shared.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

Since we do not collect or store any data on our servers, we do not retain any of your personal information. Any data the App accesses is stored locally on your device and is managed by the Android operating system. Deleting the App will remove any local data associated specifically with the App’s internal storage.

6. Children’s Privacy

Our App does not collect any information from children under the age of 13 (or 16 in certain jurisdictions), as it does not collect any data at all.
